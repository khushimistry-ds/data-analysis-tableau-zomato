# data-analysis-tableau-zomato
This repository contains a comprehensive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze and visualize restaurant data from Zomato. The project provides insights into market trends, pricing strategies, and restaurant performance across various cities in India.

# The dashboard tracks several critical metrics and KPIs:

Executive Summary Tiles: 
Provides a high-level overview of total engagement including Total Votes (787,337), Total Restaurants (6,593), Average Rating (4.088), and Average Cost (1,103).

Restaurant Volume by City: A detailed breakdown of restaurant counts across major hubs like Bangalore (1,019), Delhi (734), and Mumbai (727).

Cost Analysis: A bar chart visualizing the Average Cost by city, highlighting price variations from cities like Kochi (612.1) to Delhi (1,610.3).

Top Performing Brands: Analysis of restaurant chains by the Count of IDs, featuring brands such as Wow! Momo, Kanti Sweets, and Barbeque Nation.

Rating Distribution: A focused look at high-rated establishments, identifying top-rated names like The Shake Maker (4.68) and Mamagoto (4.49).

Geographic & Market Presence: Utilizes Tree Maps and Packed Bubble Charts to represent market share by city (e.g., Agra, Noida, Goa) and individual brand presence (e.g., Subway, Spice It).

Interactive Filtering: Includes a global City Filter to allow users to drill down into specific regional data.

Tech Stack
Data Visualization: Tableau

Data Source: Zomato Restaurant Dataset
